Point up the picture to tuck, down it to stand tall, to either side to turn: carved, or skidded out at the ring's edge. Hold Brake, or Shift, to stop; 180, or X, turns you round on the snow to switch and back (a pop, a nose butter, a smooth 180, a tail butter, in turn). The arrow keys steer too; held on, they skid. Off a kicker: tucked, you fly as a ski jumper; between, straight air; standing tall, a backflip; turning a little, a 180 with it; turning hard, a spin for as long as you hold it, stopping at the next half turn when you let go (an odd one lands you switch); tucked and turning, a cork. In the air, point up to turn quicker; up and to a side to grab (left a mute, right the tail); down to spread eagle, or lay out a flip.
