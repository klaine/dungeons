Dungeons: world

One country, six kilometres by ten, from the sea and its islands in the south across the low country and the fells to the hills in the north. The islands, the valley heads, the mountain tops and the coastal towns a house could stand on are found in it, not drawn, and marked in red; each can be gone to, a house built on it and played, or its land walked. Making a world takes half a minute or so.
